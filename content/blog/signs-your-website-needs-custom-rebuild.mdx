Template websites have their place — they're fast to launch and affordable when you're just starting out. But as a business grows, templates often start working against you instead of for you. Here's how to know when it's time for something built specifically around your business.

## 1. Your Site Looks Like Everyone Else's

Templates are, by definition, used by thousands of other businesses. If your website looks like a dozen competitors' sites with different colors and a different logo, it's not helping you stand out — it's making you forgettable.

A custom-built site is designed around your brand specifically, so it looks and feels like nobody else's.

## 2. You're Fighting the Template Instead of Using It

If you find yourself avoiding certain features, working around limitations, or accepting "good enough" instead of what you actually want — the template is dictating your business, not the other way around.

Custom development means the site is built around what you need, not the other way around.

## 3. Your Site Is Slow

Many templates and page-builder platforms load unnecessary code for features you don't even use. This shows up as slow load times — which affects both user experience and how Google ranks your site.

A custom-built site only includes what it actually needs, which typically means significantly better speed and performance.

## 4. You Need Functionality Templates Can't Handle

Booking systems, custom dashboards, integrations with other tools, chatbots, CRMs — most templates simply weren't built for this. You end up stacking plugins and workarounds that slow the site down and frequently break.

Custom development can build exactly the functionality your business needs, integrated cleanly instead of bolted on.

## 5. You've Outgrown "Good Enough"

The template that worked when you were starting out may no longer reflect where your business actually is now. If your site no longer matches the level of business you're running, it's actively working against how potential clients perceive you.

## What a Custom Rebuild Actually Solves

A custom website isn't just about looking nicer — it's about having a site that's fast, scalable, and built around your actual business needs, from SEO fundamentals to functionality that templates can't support.

If any of the signs above sound familiar, it's worth having a conversation about what a custom-built site could do for your business instead.
