## The Playing Field Just Changed

For years, professional video content was out of reach for most small businesses. A 30-second brand commercial could cost $5,000–$20,000 when you factored in crew, equipment, location, and post-production.

That's no longer true.

AI video tools have made it possible to produce scroll-stopping content — the kind that used to require a full production crew — in days, not weeks, and at a fraction of the cost.

## What AI Video Actually Is (and Isn't)

AI-generated video doesn't mean robots replacing creativity. It means using tools that can generate custom footage, animate visuals, add professional voiceovers, and build visual narratives from a script and brand direction.

The result looks polished, professional, and — when done right — completely on-brand.

What AI video **can't** replace is strategy and creative direction. The hook still needs to work. The copy still needs to convert. The visuals still need to fit your brand. That's where we come in.

## Where It Works Best

We've seen AI video work exceptionally well for:

- **Product showcases** — showing your product in context without a full photoshoot
- **Educational content** — explaining a service, procedure, or concept in 60 seconds
- **Social media reels** — fast, visual content designed for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts
- **Brand commercials** — a polished intro to your business for ads or your website

## The Real Advantage for Small Businesses

Speed and cost are the obvious benefits. But the bigger advantage is this: **you can now test content**.

Instead of spending $15,000 on one commercial and hoping it works, you can produce 3–5 different video concepts, test them, and double down on what performs. That's a competitive edge that most small businesses couldn't access before.

Traditional production locks you into one creative direction. AI video gives you options — and the ability to iterate fast based on what your audience actually responds to.
