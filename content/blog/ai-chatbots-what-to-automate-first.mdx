Not every process in your business should be automated with AI at the same time — and trying to do it all at once is often exactly why an automation project never gets off the ground. Here's where to start.

## Start with Repetitive Questions, Not Complex Conversations

A well-configured chatbot can handle "What are your hours?", "Do you ship to...?", and "How much does...?" with accuracy and consistency — freeing up hours from your team every week. That's the first win to go after.

## Automate the First Response, Not the Final Decision

The initial goal of a chatbot isn't to close the sale. It's to qualify the customer and direct them to the right person or next step — without making them wait hours for a reply. Get that right first.

## Prioritize Your Highest-Volume Channels

If most of your inquiries come through WhatsApp or Instagram, that's where a chatbot generates the most immediate impact — before you worry about secondary channels. Meet your customers where they already are.

## What You Shouldn't Automate Yet

Negotiations, sensitive complaints, and any interaction where the customer needs to feel like they're talking to a real person who understands their specific situation. Automation that makes someone feel like a ticket number is worse than no automation at all.

## The Right Way to Think About It

A good chatbot doesn't replace your team. It removes friction before your team steps in to handle what actually matters.
