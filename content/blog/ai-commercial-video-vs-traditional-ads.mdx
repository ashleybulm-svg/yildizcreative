Before committing budget to your next commercial, it's worth understanding what you're actually buying. Traditional production and AI-generated video aren't just different in price — they're different in what kind of outcome you can expect.

## Traditional Commercial Production

Camera crew, location or studio, talent, physical art direction, professional editing. It's the process most people picture when they think "video shoot."

**Strengths:**
- Full creative control over every visual detail
- Your actual product, team, or space on screen
- Ideal when the message depends on showing something tangible and specific

**Trade-offs:**
- Higher cost — even a simple shoot involves multiple people and equipment
- Timelines measured in weeks, not days
- Difficult and expensive to iterate if the first cut doesn't land

## AI Commercial Video

Concepts and scenes generated from a script and human creative direction, without a physical shoot.

**Strengths:**
- Significantly lower cost
- Delivered in days, not weeks
- Makes it realistic to test 2 or 3 different versions before deciding which one to run in paid media

**Trade-offs:**
- Less precise for showing your specific physical product or real testimonials
- The final result depends heavily on the quality of the creative direction behind it — AI doesn't compensate for a weak concept

## The Question That Actually Matters

Don't ask "which is better?" Ask: "What do I need to test or communicate?"

If you're launching a campaign and want to test messaging before committing your media budget, AI video lets you iterate fast and affordably. If your commercial needs to show your actual location, your real team, or your product in an authentic setting — traditional production is still the right call.
