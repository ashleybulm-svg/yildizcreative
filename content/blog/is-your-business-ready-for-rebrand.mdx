Rebranding isn't just changing your logo when you're tired of looking at it. It's a strategic decision — and like any strategic decision, it has a right moment and moments when it simply isn't worth pursuing yet.

## Signs It's the Right Time

**Your business changed direction** — new services, a new audience, a new model — and your current brand no longer reflects where you actually are. The outside doesn't match the inside.

**You're entering a more competitive or premium market**, and your current image doesn't compete at that level. You're showing up to a different game with the wrong gear.

**You went through a real reputation crisis** that requires a genuine reintroduction of your brand to the market — not a cosmetic update, but a meaningful repositioning.

## Signs It's Not the Right Time (Yet)

**You're just visually tired of your brand**, but your clients recognize it and trust it. Their familiarity is an asset. Don't trade it away for novelty.

**The real problem isn't your identity** — it's your sales strategy, your product, or your customer service. A new logo won't fix those.

**You don't have clarity on where you want to take the business** in the next 2–3 years. Rebranding without a clear direction is an expense, not an investment. You'll end up doing it again.

## The Question That Cuts Through It

Does your current brand represent who you and your business are *today* — or who you were when you started?

If the honest answer is the second one, it's probably time to have the conversation.
