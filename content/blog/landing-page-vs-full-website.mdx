One of the most costly mistakes when launching a business or campaign is investing in a full website when what you actually needed was a landing page — or building a landing page when your business needed a proper foundation. Here's how to decide correctly.

## What a Landing Page Is

A single page focused on one specific goal: selling a product, capturing leads for an event, promoting a limited offer. It's designed to convert without distractions.

**Best for:** paid media campaigns with a single message, validating an idea before investing further, launching something fast with a clear deadline.

## What a Full Website Is

Multiple pages (home, services, about, contact, blog) that represent your entire business and serve as the permanent foundation of your digital presence.

**Best for:** established businesses representing their full offer, organic SEO over time, industries where clients research you before they trust you.

## How to Choose

**Choose a landing page if:**
- You're running a specific campaign with a start and end date
- You're testing a business idea before committing more resources
- You need something functional in days, not weeks

**Choose a full website if:**
- Your business is established and needs to represent its complete offering
- Long-term organic search is part of your growth strategy
- Clients need to understand and trust you before they reach out

## The Reality for Most Businesses

Many businesses actually need both — at different moments. A landing page to sell now. A full website to build authority over time. The key is knowing which one serves the goal you're working toward right now.
